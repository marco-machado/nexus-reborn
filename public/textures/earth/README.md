# Earth imagery

`blue-marble.jpg` is NASA Earth Observatory's **Blue Marble: Next Generation,
July 2004, with topography and bathymetry**, supplied at 5400 × 2700 pixels.
It is a geographic equirectangular texture, not an AI-generated approximation.

- [NASA collection and credit](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry/)
- [Original JPEG](https://assets.science.nasa.gov/content/dam/science/esd/eo/images/bmng/bmng-topography-bathymetry/july/world.topo.bathy.200407.3x5400x2700.jpg)
- [NASA image-use guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/)

Country and sector outlines come from public-domain Natural Earth 1:110m
geometry, generated with `scripts/prepare-scan.py`. The globe uses the complete
latitude range, including Antarctica. The geographic Europe–Asia division is a
cartographic approximation of the Urals, Ural River, Caspian, Greater Caucasus,
and Turkish Straits convention, not a claim about political borders.

The data is bundled locally; viewing or rotating the globe makes no external
imagery requests. Fictional city names retain the game's established locations;
Pacifica is anchored at 37.6138°N, 122.4869°W, and Lagos at 6.4551°N, 3.3841°E.
