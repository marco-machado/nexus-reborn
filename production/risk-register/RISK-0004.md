# Risk: One owner reviews every decision

## Identification

- **ID**: RISK-0004
- **Identified By**: Claude, delegated by the owner's /goal 2026-10-23
- **Date Identified**: 2026-10-23
- **Category**: Resource

## Assessment

- **Probability**: High
- **Impact**: Moderate
- **Risk Score**: High

## Description

The project has one human. Under autonomous mode Claude logs and proceeds on delegated decisions; the owner reviews later. Decisions can pile up unreviewed.

## Trigger Conditions

- More than ten delegated decisions unreviewed at a sprint close
- A delegated decision reversed after work was built on it

## Impact Analysis

### If This Risk Materializes

Rework when a delegated decision is reversed.

### Affected Systems/Features

- All

## Mitigation Strategy

### Prevention (reduce probability)

- Every delegated decision is logged with its source (`Claude, delegated by …`) in the spec or story where it applies
- Owner review at each sprint close
- Owner: producer

### Contingency (reduce impact if it occurs)

- Reverse and re-route through the owning skill; record the reversal in the decision log
