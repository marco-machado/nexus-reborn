# Risk: Agent playtesters stand in for human players

## Identification

- **ID**: RISK-0001
- **Identified By**: Claude, delegated by the owner's /goal 2026-10-23
- **Date Identified**: 2026-10-23
- **Category**: Quality

## Assessment

- **Probability**: High
- **Impact**: Moderate
- **Risk Score**: High

## Description

Playtests 2–4 are played by fresh AI agents through a headless harness that holds the mission pause between commands. They see the game only through screenshots and text, never read the code, and are new to the game. They are not humans: their reading speed, patience and reaction time differ, and the held pause removes real-time pressure.

## Trigger Conditions

- A finding a human would hit is absent from every agent report
- The owner's hand-played mission (2026-10-28) disagrees with the agents on difficulty or confusion

## Impact Analysis

### If This Risk Materializes

Fun-hypothesis evidence could overstate or understate how a human feels; difficulty could read easier under the held pause.

### Affected Systems/Features

- Tactical Mission
- Interface
- World Network

## Mitigation Strategy

### Prevention (reduce probability)

- The owner's hand-played EC-023 mission by 2026-10-28 (retro 10 action 3)

- Owner: producer

### Contingency (reduce impact if it occurs)

- If a human contradicts the agents, re-open the affected playtest finding and re-route it through /playtest-report
