## Observations (not failures)

- **TC01**: the case expects post-payout Credits = start + 32,000 "±0" unless invoice lines account for it. The balance is +4,169 over that, deposited by Tax during the 2-day ETA catch-up, and no invoice line shows it. Economy GDD rule: Tax is not an invoice money line; identity `new_balance − credits_before = net_payout + tax_deposits` holds (32,000 + 4,169). Case text should be updated to that identity.
- **TC07**: step 2 as written (S-4 `setOutcome` with `quietReplay: false`) cannot produce the `REPLAY // FEE ALREADY COLLECTED` title; it prints CONTRACT FULFILLED and pays again. With `quietReplay: true` (what the deploy freeze sets for a won authored contract) the title matches and the prompt stays silent (TC07b). Case fixture should pass `quietReplay: true`.
