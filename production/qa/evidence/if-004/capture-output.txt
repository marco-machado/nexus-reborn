onboarding text: WORLD NETWORK // ORIENTATION FIRST UPLINK THE JOB You run the network from this desk. Every sector prints a Tax yield, but only a Nexus-held sector pays it: North America pays from the start, Europe does not. Keep sectors Nexus-held. START HERE Focus a few sectors and read Control, Unrest, Tax yield and Garrison. When a sector needs hands on the ground, take its contract. STRATEGIC TIME Pause and Clock speed control strategic time: Pause stops it while you read, 1x to 8x runs it faster. Labs, healing and Tax all run on it. INFLUENCE Influence opens at 0 points, so Stabilize and Lobby read UNAFFORDABLE at first, and Expedite reads NO TARGET until the sector lists a GENERATED contract to rush: they are not broken. Field wins earn the points to spend. SECTORS The left column lists every sector; pick one to focus it on the scan. Crisis states flag themselves in red. SECTOR COMMAND The right panel reads the focused sector: control, unrest, tax yield, garrison, the numbered influence actions, and the event forecast. OPEN CONTRACTS Below it, the open contracts for that sector. Opening one moves to the Brief. TIME AND EVENTS The bottom strip runs Strategic time, reviews the last 24 hours (REVIEW pins the Feed; the sector numbers stay live), and carries the Feed and your resource pool. RESEARCH The RESEARCH tab on the bottom navigation opens the lab programs; labs run on Strategic time. UNDERSTOOD
